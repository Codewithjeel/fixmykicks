Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order on FixMyKicks?

Simply select your UK size on any shoe card and click Order Now. It will open WhatsApp with your selected shoe name, product code, price, and size pre-filled. You can also add multiple shoes to your WhatsApp Bag and send all of them together in a single WhatsApp message.

How many days does delivery take across India?

For Delhi NCR, same-day or next-day delivery is available. For all other cities across India (Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, etc.), courier delivery takes 3–5 working days with live tracking shared on WhatsApp.

Can I see real photos or a video of my shoe on WhatsApp before ordering?

Yes! Click on any shoe to open its details and click "Request Live Photo / Video on WhatsApp", or message us directly on WhatsApp and our Delhi desk will share live visuals of your exact size.

What if I am unsure about my UK shoe size?

Click on UK Size Chart at the top of the page to compare UK, Euro (EU), and foot length in centimeters (CM). You can also message our team on WhatsApp and we will guide you on the exact fit for each brand (Nike, Adidas, On Cloud, Birkenstock, Crocs, New Balance, Puma, etc.).
